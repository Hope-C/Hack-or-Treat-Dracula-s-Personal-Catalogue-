Section 02 // Character Case Study

Problem, Cause & Day-to-Day Task

Why does an immortal Transylvanian Count desperately need a custom software application just to get dressed?

01. The Problem I

He Can’t See Himself in the Mirror

Because Count Dracula is an undead vampire, traditional silver-backed glass mirrors reflect nothing at all. When he stands in front of his dressing-room mirror, he only sees the empty stone wall behind him.

  • ✕ Cannot tell if his silk cravat is crooked
  • ✕ Cannot see if his cape clashes with his vest
  • ✕ Cannot check how a new outfit fits his frame
Status: Optical Reflection = 0%
02. The Cause II

Weekly Parties & Nobleman Prestige

Dracula loves throwing lavish castle parties every single week. Because he is a proud, high-ranking nobleman, aristocratic etiquette strictly forbids him from wearing the same outfit to every party!

  • ★ Hosts 52 grand castle galas per year
  • ★ Must debut a fresh, regal ensemble every week
  • ★ Repeating an outfit ruins his noble reputation
Rule: Zero Outfit Repeats Allowed
03. Day-to-Day Task III

Dressing Up for His Parties

Before the castle gates open each week, Dracula must mix-and-match capes, brocade waistcoats, medallions, and top hats—and verify that the complete outfit looks regal before greeting his guests.

  • ✓ Coordinate cape, vest, and cravat colors
  • ✓ Preview the complete look in a virtual mirror
  • ✓ Log each week’s outfit so it isn’t repeated
Goal: Flawless Weekly Party Attire
Section 03 // System Specification

Function, Objective & Coding Languages

How our custom software architecture bridges undead folklore with modern web and backend programming.

F
System Function

Help With Daily Tasks (Party Wardrobe & Grooming)

Assists Dracula in assembling, aligning, and rating his party outfits each week. It automatically checks whether an ensemble matches the party’s dress code and alerts him if he already wore that combination at a previous party.

O
Core Objective

Help Him See What He Looks Like in the Mirror

Replaces his useless silver-backed glass mirror with a digital Sartorial Smart-Mirror. By combining a Python wardrobe engine with an HTML/CSS visual canvas, Dracula can finally see a full-color reflection of himself and his outfit!

Python 3.x (Backend Logic) dracula_mirror.py

Wardrobe Engine & Non-Repeat Algorithm

Stores Dracula’s royal wardrobe inventory, computes color-harmony & nobleman prestige scores, tracks past weekly party outfits in a history set so he never wears the same outfit twice, and exports the active reflection state.

HTML5 (Structure & SVG) index.html

Smart-Mirror Portal & Layered Avatar

Structures the interactive dressing-room interface, the weekly party selector, and the multi-layered SVG vector portrait of Count Dracula (cape, waistcoat, cravat, medallion, and facial reflection).

CSS3 (Gothic Styling) styles.css

Gilded Frame, Velvet & Reflection FX

Crafts the arched antique-gold mirror frame, flickering candlelight animations, damask velvet textures, and smooth opacity transitions between “Invisible Silver Mirror” and “Synthesized Smart-Mirror”.
